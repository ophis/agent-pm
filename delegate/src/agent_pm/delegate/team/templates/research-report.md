# Report: [Reference] [Title]

Title: no gloss; `[Title]` never repeats `[Reference]`. Combining several reports: each id appears once, in its sub-report's heading. One fact per list item; its reason goes in the synthesis or under Gaps, not on that line.

Deep research's type line (light research gives its own): the type; for local or mixed, each `<repo>` at `<commit>`.

## Conclusion and recommendation
Opens with one sentence answering the question, with an overall confidence. Then list items: the findings it rests on and the recommendation, labeled as your synthesis.

## Comparison table
Optional; omit when the input asks for no comparison. A table ([Principles › Writing › Diagrams and tables](#writing)), labeled as your synthesis.

## Findings
One subsection per part of the question; one describing an architecture, flow or timeline gets a diagram ([Principles › Writing › Diagrams and tables](#writing)). One list item per finding, ending with a tag, translated: `(<confidence: high, medium or low>; [n]…; <single-source or unverified, when so>)`. Sources disagree → name both and which you favor, and why; confidence at most medium.

## Corrections to known claims
Optional; omit when the input states no known claims. One line per claim: the claim → the correction, its sources (`[n]`).

## Gaps
Optional; omit when none. One line per uncovered, unverified, refuted or open point, saying whether it could change the conclusion; those that could first.

## Sources
A numbered list in order of first citation, its numbers the in-text `[n]`: `1. <URL or code permalink> (primary|secondary|code)`.
